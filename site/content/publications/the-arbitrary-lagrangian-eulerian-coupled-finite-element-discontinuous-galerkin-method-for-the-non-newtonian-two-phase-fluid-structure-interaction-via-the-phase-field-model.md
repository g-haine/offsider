## Abstract
In this work, we establish a coupled finite element-discontinuous Galerkin (FEM-DG) method in the arbitrary Lagrangian–Eulerian (ALE) computational framework for the challenging non-Newtonian two phase fluid structure interaction problems. The moving interface between two different fluids is described via the phase field model. The flow field governing equations are written in a unified form according to the ALE description. The elastodynamics equation of the solid is written in the Lagrangian formulation. The numerical solution of non-Newtonian two phase Navier–Stokes equations is accomplished via the coupled finite element and discontinuous Galerkin method. The Cahn–Hilliard equation is solved according to the streamline upwind Petrov–Galerkin (SUPG) method and the governing equation of solid structure is solved based on the standard finite element method. The deformation of the fluid domain mesh is managed using a mesh moving technique. A semi-implicit partitioned coupling scheme is designed to combine these four solvers efficiently and stably. The flow through a fixed cylinder with an elastic rod is investigated to demonstrate the convergence and validity of our combined computational algorithm. It is the first time that the non-Newtonian two phase dam break flow impacting a flexible rod is studied in detail. The development of the interface between two fluids and the deformation of the elastic rod with different Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids are all fully analyzed.
