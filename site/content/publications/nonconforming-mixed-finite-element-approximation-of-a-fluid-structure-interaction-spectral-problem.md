## Abstract
We aim to provide a finite element analysis for the elastoacoustic vibration problem.We use a dual-mixed variational formulation for the elasticity systemand combine the lowest order Lagrange finiteelement in the fluid domain with the reduced symmetry element known as PEERSand introduced for linear elasticity in [1]. We show that the resultingglobal nonconforming scheme provides a correct spectral approximationand we prove quasi-optimal error estimates.Finally, we confirm the asymptotic rates of convergence by numerical experiments.
