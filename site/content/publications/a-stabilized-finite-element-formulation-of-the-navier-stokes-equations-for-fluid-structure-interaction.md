## Abstract
This paper presents a space-time finite element formulation of the Navier-Stokes equations, stabilized via the Galerkin/least-squares method. The variational equation is based on the time discontinuous Galerkin method and is written in terms of the physical entropy variables. This formulation is analogous to the arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian technique and thus is applicable to fluid-structure interaction problems. Free surface oscillation of a liquid filled tank is presented to show the performance of the proposed method.
