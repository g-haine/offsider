## Abstract
The aim of this paper is to provide a survey of the state of the art in thefinite element approach to the Immersed Boundary Method (FE-IBM) which hasbeen investigated by the authors during the last decade. In a unified setting,we present the different formulation proposed in our research and highlightthe advantages of the one based on a distributed Lagrange multiplier(DLM-IBM) over the original FE-IBM.
