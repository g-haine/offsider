## Abstract
We address a system of equations modeling an incompressible fluid interacting with an elastic body. We prove the local existence when the initial velocity belongs to the space $$H^{1.5+\epsilon }$$ H 1.5 + ϵ and the initial structure velocity is in $$H^{1+\epsilon }$$ H 1 + ϵ , where $$\epsilon \in (0, 1/20)$$ ϵ ∈ ( 0 , 1 / 20 ) .
