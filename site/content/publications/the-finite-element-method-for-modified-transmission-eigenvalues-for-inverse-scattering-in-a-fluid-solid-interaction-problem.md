## Abstract
Based on the work of Monk and Selgas (2022), we explore the finite element method for the modified transmission eigenvalues for inverse scattering in a fluid-solid interaction problem. We give a complete error analysis including the a priori and the a posteriori error estimates. We also present adaptive computations.
